Rules

8 teams will compete across 4 playdays to gain points for the Boolin League Season 1 Major.

The first 8 teams to sign up will be confirmed. However, if a team cannot make a playday, a substitute team will be able to play and earn points for the leaderboard.

Points are distributed based on tournament placement

1st Place 12 pts
2nd Place 7 pts
3rd / 4th Place 3 pts
5th – 8th Place 0 pts
